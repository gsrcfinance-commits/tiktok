## Privacy Policy **Global SimRacing Channel — Internal Analytics Tool — Privacy Policy** *Last updated: [18/08/2026]* This application ("gsrc-socials-analytics") is an internal tool used by Global SimRacing Channel (GSRC) to read performance statistics (view count, like count, comment count, share count) for videos posted to GSRC's own TikTok account (@globalsimracingchannel). **What it accesses:** Only the GSRC TikTok account's own basic profile information and its own posted video list and statistics, via TikTok's official API, authorized directly by GSRC. **What it does not do:** This tool does not access any other TikTok user's data. It does not collect, store, or process data from TikTok viewers, followers, or any third party. It does not post, comment, or modify content on TikTok. **Data storage:** Statistics retrieved are stored locally within GSRC's internal systems for the sole purpose of internal weekly performance reporting. Data is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. **Contact:** For questions about this policy, contact gsrcfinance@gmail.com.
